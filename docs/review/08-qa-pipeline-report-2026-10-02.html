What this cannot tell you yet

  • The after window is 24 runs over 8 commits in about 8 hours. That is enough to see the infrastructure errors stop; it is not enough to state a flake rate. A week of runs would settle it.
  • No commit after the fixes was rerun, so pass-on-rerun cannot be compared (8 of 36 failing commit/job pairs passed on rerun before).
  • Review-round numbers after the cutoff (mean 1.9) cover tickets still in flight, so they are not an improvement yet. The review-process tickets have not merged.
  • Windows sharding (L-0577) has not had its first CI run, so the projected 12–15 minute Windows job is an estimate.
