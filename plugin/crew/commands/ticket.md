`/crew:ticket` was removed in crew 1.0. It is not an alias and does nothing.
Use `/crew:brainstorm` to mint the ticket and settle a direction, then `/crew:spec`.
The 1.0 lifecycle continues `/crew:plan`, `/crew:implement`, `/crew:review`, `/crew:done`.
Tell the user that, and stop.
